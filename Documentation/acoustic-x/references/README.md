# Acoustic X — the suggested references, read

The 1997 *Acoustic-X* manual closes with a page of 26 **Suggested references** (chapter 9,
`manual-1997/09 Suggested references.pdf`). This folder holds one book report per
entry, in the manual's order and with its numbering.

Each report gives the citation as the manual printed it (with any correction noted), says
what the work is and why Acoustic-X pointed at it, and then takes its **big ideas** one at a
time. Every big idea has:

- **Summary:** what the work says, in plain words, with the equation where there is one.
- **Challenge:** something to do that tests the idea, by hand or in the app's Acoustic X
  windows, and what you should find.

A closing **Since then** says what later work kept, revised or dropped, and **In this app**
names the chapter and the function that carry the idea, where one does.

These are reports, not copies. No text from the works is reproduced beyond a short quote,
and the reports were written from the published record of each work, not from scans of it.
